Services

Eight service lines, explained straight.

Residential heating and cooling is the core: diagnostics, repairs, maintenance, installations and replacements. Here is what each service involves and when you need it.

  • Furnaces
  • Air conditioning
  • Installations
  • Maintenance
  • Thermostats
  • Mini-splits
  • Ductwork
  • Light commercial

  • Furnace diagnostics, troubleshooting and repairs

    A furnace that won't start, shuts off early, blows cool air or has started making new noises usually has a specific cause, such as an ignition or flame-sensing problem, a blower fault, restricted airflow or a control issue. Diagnostics means finding that cause before any parts get replaced. The repair follows from what's found.

    When you'd need it: no heat, uneven heat, a furnace that cycles on and off, or anything that smells, sounds or behaves differently than it used to.

  • Air conditioning diagnostics, troubleshooting and repairs

    Cooling problems tend to show up as warm air from the vents, a system that runs constantly without catching up, ice on the lines, or water where it shouldn't be. Troubleshooting covers the outdoor unit, the indoor coil, airflow, drainage and the controls to pin down what's actually failing.

    When you'd need it: the system runs but the house stays warm, it won't turn on at all, or it trips the breaker or leaks.

  • Heating and cooling system installations and replacements

    Installing a new system, or replacing a furnace or air conditioner, means choosing equipment that suits the building, removing the old unit, and connecting the new one to the existing ductwork, electrical, fuel and drainage. What a replacement involves depends on what's there now, so Amar talks through the job with you first.

    When you'd need it: equipment that has failed or keeps needing repairs, a renovation or addition, or a building that doesn't have the heating or cooling it needs.

  • Preventative maintenance and seasonal tune-ups

    A tune-up is a scheduled look at a system while it's still working, so that worn or dirty parts are found and dealt with before they cause a breakdown. The point is to catch small problems before the season when you depend on the equipment.

    When you'd need it: heating equipment before the cold months, cooling equipment before summer, and on a recurring basis for rental properties.

  • Thermostat installation

    The thermostat is what tells your equipment when to run, so it has to be matched and wired correctly to the system it controls. Installation covers mounting, wiring, setup and checking that heating and cooling both respond properly.

    When you'd need it: a thermostat that has stopped working or reads wrong, an upgrade to a different model, or new equipment that needs a compatible control.

  • Mini-split installation and service

    A mini-split pairs an outdoor unit with one or more indoor units mounted in the rooms they serve, connected by refrigerant lines instead of ducts. That makes it a practical way to heat and cool spaces where running ductwork isn't realistic. Service covers diagnosing and repairing units that are already installed.

    When you'd need it: additions, finished attics and basements, older homes without ducts, or single rooms that are always too hot or too cold.

  • Ductwork and ventilation

    Ducts carry heated and cooled air from the equipment to each room and bring it back again. Leaks, disconnected runs and undersized or poorly laid-out ducts leave rooms uncomfortable no matter how well the equipment works. Ventilation work deals with moving stale air out and fresh air in.

    When you'd need it: rooms that never reach temperature, weak airflow at the vents, a renovation that changes the layout, or new equipment that the old ductwork doesn't suit.

  • Select light commercial HVAC

    Yugo Contracting is a residential-first company that takes on select light commercial HVAC work, including maintenance and service for local businesses. Whether a commercial job is a fit depends on its scope, and that's decided when Amar talks it through with you.

    When you'd need it: a local business looking for maintenance or service on its heating and cooling. More for local businesses.
