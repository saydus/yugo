Why an owner-operator

The owner does the work and answers for it.

Yugo Contracting is one person, Amar Sarajlija. For a property manager or a builder, that is the point.

  • Accountability

    The person who scopes the work is the person who does it and the person who answers for it.

  • One contact

    One name and one direct line for every property and every project.

  • Consistent workmanship

    The same hands on every unit, so the work is done the same way across your portfolio.

  • No handoffs

    Nothing passes between a salesperson, a dispatcher and a technician, because there is only Amar.

Property managers & landlords

Maintenance, repairs and replacements across your residential properties.

Whether you manage a portfolio or own a few rentals, the heating and cooling in every unit is your problem when it stops. This is the work Yugo Contracting does for you.

  • One point of contactYou deal with Amar directly for every property.
  • Recurring maintenance across a portfolioPreventative maintenance and seasonal tune-ups planned across the properties you look after, instead of one breakdown at a time.
  • RepairsFurnace and air conditioning diagnostics, troubleshooting and repairs when a unit has a problem.
  • Turnover and replacement workEquipment replacements when a system has reached the end of its life, including between tenants when a unit is empty.

Look after residential properties? Set up an account as a property manager or a landlord.

Real estate investors

Rental properties, renovations and acquisitions.

When you're buying, renovating or holding property, heating and cooling is one of the systems that has to work before anyone moves in.

  • Rental propertiesDiagnostics, repairs and maintenance on the equipment in the properties you hold.
  • RenovationsSystem installations and replacements, ductwork and ventilation, mini-splits and thermostats as part of a renovation.
  • AcquisitionsHeating and cooling work on properties you're taking on.
  • One point of contactThe same person for each property: Amar.

Buying, renovating or holding property? Set up an account as an investor.

General contractors & builders

Subcontracting on HVAC installs and replacements.

You run the project. Yugo Contracting comes in as the HVAC subcontractor for the heating and cooling scope.

  • Installs and replacementsHeating and cooling system installations and replacements as a subcontractor on your jobs.
  • The rest of the HVAC scopeDuctwork and ventilation, mini-split installation and thermostat installation.
  • You deal with the ownerScope is discussed with Amar directly.
  • WhereUtica, Rome, Herkimer, Syracuse and surrounding communities. Other nearby areas depending on the scope and size of the job.

Need an HVAC subcontractor? Set up an account as a builder or GC.

Local businesses

Light commercial maintenance and service.

Yugo Contracting is a residential-first company that takes on select light commercial HVAC work.

  • MaintenancePreventative maintenance on the heating and cooling equipment your business depends on.
  • ServiceDiagnostics and repairs when something isn't working.
  • Depends on scopeNot every commercial job is a fit. Tell Amar about the building and the equipment when he calls.

Run a local business? Set up an account as a local business.

Set up an account

Start with your details. Amar takes it from there.

Setting up an account starts the conversation about ongoing work. It's a request for a call back, not a booking.

  1. Amar gets your request

    Who you are and what kind of work you need. The rest is optional.

  2. He calls you himself to talk it through

    Your properties or projects, and what you need. Pricing is discussed directly with him once he knows the work.

  3. He sets up the visit

    Work gets scheduled with you from there.
